UAS Remote Pilot — Open Category A1 / A3

Online training course and theoretical examination required by EU Regulation 2019/947 for anyone operating a drone in the European UAS Open Category A1 or A3.

Air Safety Airspace Rules Aviation Regulation Human Factors Privacy & GDPR

What you'll learn

  • Preparing yourself and your drone for flightChecklists, weight categories, equipment limits.
  • Deciding when it is safe to fly — and when it is notWeather, airspace, environment and crowd assessment.
  • Flying in a way that respects the rulesOperational procedures, distance and altitude limits, registration.
  • Reacting to abnormal and emergency situationsIn-flight failures, ground emergencies, fly-aways and reporting.
  • Privacy, data protection, insurance and securityPersonal data, third-party liability, anti-collision and security duties.

Audience: remote pilots wishing to operate in the European UAS Open Category A1 and/or A3.
Important: the examination is locked after successful completion. The certificate can be downloaded from your "Remote Pilot Certificates" account.

📝 How it works

How to join a Summer School cohort

Three steps. Study groups are members-only — we organise, you study, the official body certifies.

1️⃣

Become a member

Active TechLegion association members get free access to all Summer School study groups. Apply here — or pass the Claude exam by 1 Sept 2026 to get membership free for 2026.

2️⃣

Reserve your seat

If you're already a member, just fill in the form for the specific cohort. TechLegion pre-registers you on the official study platform.

3️⃣

Study and get certified

TechLegion pre-registers you on the online platform — you get a personal cabinet with progress tracking and interactive courses. Complete the basic courses to unlock the exam, attend live group sessions to practise with real cases, and the certificate is issued by the authorising body under your legal name once you pass.
