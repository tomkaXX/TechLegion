TechLegion Ambassadors

TechLegion Ambassadors are technology professionals, researchers and innovators who carry our mission beyond Switzerland's borders. They bridge communities, open doors to world-class facilities, bring global perspectives into the Swiss tech ecosystem — and inspire the next generation of builders through their expertise and example.

Every ambassador represents a different domain, country and career path — but they share one conviction: that open, cross-border collaboration is how breakthrough technology gets built. By connecting TechLegion members to their networks, mentoring emerging talent, and co-creating events and programs, our ambassadors are the human infrastructure that makes the community thrive.

Our ambassadors excel in their professional fields, and we're proud to have them with us — the high standards they hold themselves to help define the standards of our community as well.
