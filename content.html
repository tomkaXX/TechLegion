🌐 Community guidelines

Community Content Rules

A shared space built on respect, quality, and trust. Please read before participating.

💬

Chat Access

Our WhatsApp community has different spaces for different levels of engagement:

🔓 Open Chat: free, no membership required. Everyone is welcome.
🔐 Member Chats: exclusive spaces for active TechLegion association members.
📚 Study & Tour Groups: closed groups for specific cohorts and programmes.
✍️

Content Quality

Every message you send represents you in front of the whole group. Make it count.

  • ✅ Share content that is relevant to the chat's topic.
  • 🚫 No spam: do not send repeated, off-topic, or promotional messages.
  • 🚫 No flooding: avoid sending many short messages in quick succession.
  • 🚫 Do not forward unrelated content from other chats or external sources into group chats.
