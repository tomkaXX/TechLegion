Why become a member

TechLegion membership is built around four things: learning, access, community and opportunity. Here's everything that comes with your card.

🎓

Free certification study groups

Members-only preparation cohorts for industry certifications in AI, Robotics, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Data and emerging technologies — facilitated by working professionals, free of charge.

⚡

Fast-track event applications

Skip the waitlist. Members get priority registration and reserved seats at our capped-capacity workshops, hackathons, tech tours and offline meetups across Switzerland.

🎉

Free Summer Party

Complimentary entry to the official TechLegion Summer Party — our flagship community celebration of the year.

🤍

Members-only gatherings

Partner perks & discounts

Discounted access to partner conferences, cloud credits, co-working day passes, NASA Space Apps tracks and selected paid certifications.

❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to live in Switzerland to be a member?

No. Most of our community is based in Switzerland, but membership is open to anyone working with or interested in the Swiss tech ecosystem.

How are the free study groups organised?

Each cohort is led by a working professional or a senior member. They usually run for 4 to 10 weeks — sometimes as a short 1–3 day intensive — and mix online sessions with in-person meetups. Groups prepare you for a specific certification (e.g. Anthropic Claude AI Architect, Drone Pilot Open Category A1/A3, and other tech fundamentals courses). Certificates are issued by the official authorising body under your legal name. TechLegion only organises the study group and pre-registers you for the course. Members get a spot for free; non-members cannot join these cohorts.

What does "fast-track event applications" mean in practice?

For any capped event, members get a private registration link 48 hours before public sign-up opens, plus a reserved member quota of seats. You effectively skip the waitlist.

How exclusive are the tech ambassador events?

Very. They're invite-only gatherings with limited seats (usually 8–15 people) hosted with tech ambassadors, founders and technology leaders. Active members in good standing are added to a rotating invitation pool so everyone gets a turn over the year.

Can I cancel my membership?

Anytime, in writing, with effect from the end of the current calendar year. We don't lock people in.

How do you handle my data?

We process membership data only for managing your membership, billing and communications you opted into. See our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown — we follow Swiss FADP and GDPR.
