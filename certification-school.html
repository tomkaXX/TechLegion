📝 How it works

How to get certified with TechLegion

Three steps, all year round. We organise, you study, the official body certifies.

1️⃣

Become a member

Foundations and Professional both require a TechLegion Standard membership — Professional is also open through an accepted Joint Development Partner project. Apply here.

2️⃣

Fill in the form for your track

Each track has its own form. We verify your membership and, for the Claude exams, send you a personalised invitation email to register for the exam.

3️⃣

Schedule and pass within 15 days

From the day your invitation is issued you have 15 days to schedule and complete the exam — otherwise your exam account is deactivated. The certificate is issued by the authorising body under your legal name once you pass.

❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for Claude Certified Architect – Foundations?

Only TechLegion community members. You need an active Standard membership first; once it's verified, we send you a personalised email to register for the exam.

Who can apply for Claude Certified Architect – Professional?

TechLegion members with an active TechLegion Standard membership. Alternatively, you can apply through the Joint Development Partner track with a real production project.

What is the 15-day rule?

Your personalised exam invitation email is active for 15 calendar days from the day it is issued. Within that window you must both schedule and complete the exam. If you don't, your exam account is deactivated. The rule applies to both Claude exams and to the Joint Development Partner track.

What is the Joint Development Partner track?

You develop a real project that runs in production, with TechLegion as joint development partner, to showcase your skills. If your project is accepted, you can take the Claude Certified Architect – Professional exam as a TechLegion partner. Describe your project in the Professional application form.

How much do the exams cost?

The exam fees are set by Anthropic: $125 USD for Foundations and $175 USD for Professional, per attempt. The UAS A1/A3 drone course and exam are free, and the study group is free for members.

Who issues the certificates?

The official authorising body — Anthropic for the Claude exams, EUROCONTROL for the drone certificate. Certificates are valid for 12 months (Claude exams) and are issued under your legal name. TechLegion organises access and study support only.

What happened to the Summer School?

Summer School 2026 has ended and its promotion is closed. The Certification School replaces it and runs all year round — there are no seasonal deadlines any more.
