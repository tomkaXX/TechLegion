🛰️ What is it?

The world's largest annual global hackathon

NASA Space Apps Challenge is a hackathon for coders, scientists, designers, storytellers, makers, builders, technologists and innovators from around the world. Over one weekend, participants use free and open data from NASA and its Space Agency Partners to build solutions to real challenges on Earth and in space. It's free to take part, and no prior space or coding experience is required to join a team.

Zurich has hosted a local edition since 2024, run by a community of volunteer organizers, mentors and judges — TechLegion is proud to support and take part in the Zurich community.

❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to take part?

Yes. NASA Space Apps Challenge has no participation fees — registration and entry are free worldwide, including at the Zurich local event.

Do I need to be a TechLegion member to join?

No. NASA Space Apps Challenge Zurich is open to anyone — you register directly through spaceappschallenge.org. TechLegion is a community partner and supporter of the Zurich event, not a gatekeeper for entry.

What's the difference between Virtual and In-Person?

Local events are typically hybrid: some participants attend a physical venue, others join and collaborate remotely. Exact venue and format details for Zurich 2026 are published on the official local event page.

How do I sign up specifically for the Zurich event?

Register at spaceappschallenge.org/2026, then search the local events map and select Zurich, Switzerland as your local event before joining or forming a team.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Teams are made up of coders, designers, scientists, storytellers and builders of all kinds — non-technical skills like communication, research and design are just as valuable to a team.
