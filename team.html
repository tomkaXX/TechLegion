About TechLegion

TechLegion is a community of technology enthusiasts, builders, and innovators dedicated to learning, collaboration, and open-source contributions. The community is known for organizing the NASA Space Apps Challenge hackathons in Zurich in 2024 and 2025, bringing together participants from diverse backgrounds to solve real-world challenges. TechLegion also actively supports open-source ecosystems through translation and localization efforts for projects such as PX4 and other technology initiatives, helping make cutting-edge tools more accessible to global communities.
