🏭 Study Tours

Study Tours

Behind-the-scenes visits to leading technology facilities across Switzerland and Europe — exclusively for TechLegion members and friends. Priority registration for association members.

✓ Completed
⚛️

Kernkraftwerk Beznau & Zwillag — Axpo

19 June 2026 · Döttingen, Aargau

An exclusive behind-the-scenes visit to Switzerland's oldest nuclear power station (KKB) and the adjacent Zwillag low- and intermediate-level radioactive waste facility, hosted by Axpo. Our group toured the control room mock-up, turbine hall and waste conditioning plant — a rare look at Switzerland's nuclear energy infrastructure.

✓ Completed
⚛️

Kernkraftwerk Gösgen-Däniken

25 July 2026 · Däniken, Solothurn

A guided visit to Kernkraftwerk Gösgen (KKG), one of Switzerland's most efficient pressurised water reactors with over 45 years of operation. An unmissable opportunity for anyone interested in nuclear energy, Swiss energy infrastructure and the future of low-carbon power.

✓ Completed
🔬

CERN — European Organisation for Nuclear Research

18 July 2026 · Meyrin, Geneva

Join us for a guided visit to CERN, the world's largest particle physics laboratory and home of the Large Hadron Collider. We'll explore the accelerator complex, visit a detector experiment, and hear from researchers working at the frontier of science.
